## 02 / One system. Four controlled surfaces.

> **Web sells. ClaimFlow proves. ORPA controls. DecisionPackage verifies.**

<details>
<summary><b>Inspect the surface boundaries</b></summary>

| Surface | Role |
|---|---|
| **Web** | Explains the product, the workflow, and the proof boundary. |
| **ClaimFlow** | Organizes a messy claim into evidence-linked, review-ready work. |
| **ORPA** | Gives an identity-bound human the approve / reject control surface. |
| **DecisionPackage** | Preserves who decided what, from which evidence, and when. |

The objective is not autonomous authority. It is a controlled, auditable operating loop that helps people move faster while remaining responsible for consequential decisions.

</details>

## 04 / Operating law

> **The system drafts. A named human approves.**
>
> **Agents may become more capable. They may never give themselves more authority.**

1. **The system drafts. A named human approves.**
2. **Agents may become more capable. They may never give themselves more authority.**
3. **Money-facing and customer-facing actions require evidence and a reconstructable Decision Package.**
4. **Synthetic demonstrations are labeled as synthetic.**
5. **No accuracy, ROI, certification, production, or customer claims without proof from real work.**

## 05 / Proof boundary

The question is not whether the software looks impressive. The question is whether a person can inspect the evidence, own the decision, and stand behind it later.

<details>
<summary><b>What each surface actually proves</b></summary>

| What you see | What it proves |
|---|---|
| **Live page** | The public surface loads. |
| **Interactive demo** | The workflow can be inspected with synthetic data. |
| **Agent output** | Automation prepared work; it did not authorize the outcome. |
| **Decision Package** | The decision, authority, evidence, and context can be reconstructed. |
| **Real job + named reviewer** | The standard that matters. |

</details>
