## The first proving ground

A contractor does not lose claims because they lack photos. They lose them because photos, estimates, weather, notes, and pricing never become one package a named person can stand behind.

<p align="center">
  <picture>
    <source media="(max-width: 600px)" srcset="./assets/claimflow-workflow-mobile.png">
    <img src="./assets/claimflow-workflow.png" alt="ClaimFlow: intake, evidence-linked draft, human gate, locked Decision Package" width="100%">
  </picture>
</p>

| Step | What happens | Who owns it |
|---|---|---|
| **Gather** | Photos, files, notes, and claim context enter one case. | The job |
| **Draft** | AI organizes gaps and an evidence-linked package. | The system |
| **Gate** | A named human approves, rejects, or returns the work. | The person |
| **Lock** | Decision, reviewer, evidence, and context stay together. | The record |
| **Learn** | Verified results can inform the next cycle. | The owner |

The public [ClaimFlow demo](https://orpaynter.ai/claimflow) is synthetic. Dollars, drafts, and evidence rows there are fabricated so the gate can be walked without a live claim.

## Operating law

1. The system drafts. A named human approves.
2. Agents may get more competent. They may never give themselves more authority.
3. Money-facing and customer-facing actions need a reconstructable Decision Package plus evidence.
4. Synthetic demonstrations stay labeled synthetic.
5. Checkout is not paid. Paid is not entitled. A package existing is not a package approved.
6. Evolution may be automated. Authority escalation may not be self-approved.
7. No accuracy, ROI, headcount, certification, uptime, or “investor ready” claims without proof from real work.
