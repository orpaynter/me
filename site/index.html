04 / Non-negotiable

Operating law.

Five constraints. No quiet expansion of authority.

Open each law to inspect how this companion page respects it.

01The system drafts. A named human approves.

This page demonstrates the gate; it cannot approve work. A selected step is only a view.

02Agents may become more capable. They may never give themselves more authority.

Moving through this demonstration does not grant the page or an agent permission to act.

03Money-facing and customer-facing actions require evidence and a reconstructable Decision Package.

No money-facing or customer-facing action runs here. The illustrated record is not a real Decision Package.

04Synthetic demonstrations are labeled as synthetic.

The stepper and its illustrative states are explicitly synthetic, not real case data.

05No accuracy, ROI, certification, production, or customer claims without proof from real work.

This page makes no such claims. A polished interface is not proof of a real-world outcome.

05 / Inspect, don't assume

Proof boundary.

The question is not whether the software looks impressive. The question is whether a person can inspect the evidence, own the decision, and stand behind it later.
